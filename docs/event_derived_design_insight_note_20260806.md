## Adopted design principles

- establish a meaningful baseline before claiming improvement;
- account for operational context and confounders;
- measure magnitude and persistence, not activity alone;
- group repeated signals into actionable incidents rather than alerting on
  every event;
- move from fixed intervention schedules towards condition-based decisions;
- evaluate the complete human workflow;
- keep domain experts responsible for consequential decisions;
- treat APIs, MCP, models, and agents as infrastructure rather than value by
  themselves;
- preserve permission inheritance, traceability, and human approval.

## EOP-0001 implications

The electrician pilot is positioned as quote-outcome decision support, not as
another reminder product. Public-form responses remain hold-only. Direct
interviews and concrete actions remain the immediate first-party evidence
routes. Pilot telemetry is governed in a separate operational measurement lane
and cannot be silently relabelled to increase the primary evidence count.
