# EOP-0001 Public Validation Form Specification v0.2

## Purpose

Collect bounded first-party discovery signals about quotation follow-up and
outcome classification. Public-form narrative responses remain `hold_only`
under the current evidence taxonomy.

## Positioning shown to participants

"This short research form explores how UK electricians and small contractors
track quotation outcomes, decide what to follow up, and avoid wasting time on
opportunities that are no longer active. It is not an automated messaging or
sales service."

## Questions

1. Participant role: electrician owner, electrical contractor staff, customer
   or client, or other trade owner.
2. Approximate quotations issued in a typical month, using a range.
3. Which outcomes are currently recorded: accepted, declined, deferred,
   expired, unclassified, or not recorded.
4. Approximate proportion left without a recorded outcome, using a range.
5. Current follow-up method.
6. Main reasons follow-up is inconsistent.
7. Information that would help prioritise which quotation needs attention.
8. Concerns about a decision-support workflow.
9. Whether the participant is willing to join a pilot or waitlist.
10. Whether the participant would consider a low-cost paid tracker or service.

## Feedback reason codes

Where a participant tests a prototype, record one or more bounded reasons:
missing source data, incorrect source data, wrong classification, unsupported
inference, irrelevant recommendation, permission failure, poor presentation,
human-process failure, useful recommendation, or no feedback recorded.

## Data minimisation

Do not export names, email addresses, telephone numbers, postal addresses, IP
addresses, free-text contact details, raw transcripts, recordings, or customer
records into the Stage 4G evidence pipeline. Contact collection for a waitlist
must remain in a separate access-controlled system and be represented in the
pipeline only by a pseudonymous token and recorded action.
