# EOP-0001 Quote-Outcome Measurement Plan

## Positioning

The pilot is a governed quote-outcome decision-support workflow. It is not an
autonomous reminder sender and does not claim that follow-up alone causes more
sales.

## Primary operational question

Does the intervention reduce unclassified quotation outcomes and improve the
speed and quality of outcome recording without creating excessive follow-up,
customer confusion, or additional staff burden?

## Baseline

Record at least fourteen days before intervention and at least five eligible
quotation observations. Prefer a longer window when quotation volume is low.

Capture:

- quotations issued;
- accepted, declined, deferred, expired, and unclassified outcomes;
- time to recorded decision;
- quote-value band;
- job type;
- lead source where available without direct identifiers;
- new versus repeat customer;
- staff time per quotation;
- known changes in price, workload, staffing, channel, or process.

## Intervention

Document exactly what changed. Examples include receipt confirmation,
classification prompts, a human-approved follow-up schedule, expiry close-out,
or a dashboard that prioritises unresolved high-value quotations.

## Observation

Record at least fourteen days after intervention. A response immediately after
a reminder is not treated as durable improvement. Persistence must be measured
within the observation window.

## Core metrics

The primary metric is `unclassified_rate_percent`.

Supporting metrics include:

- `median_time_to_decision_days`;
- `staff_minutes_per_quote`;
- `decline_reason_capture_rate_percent`;
- `deferred_rate_percent`;
- `unnecessary_followups_count`;
- `accepted_rate_percent`;
- `gross_margin_estimate_gbp`.

Accepted-rate or margin changes must not be described as caused by the
intervention without a stronger comparison design and control of confounders.

## Interpretation

Report magnitude, direction, persistence, confounders, unintended effects, and
sample size. Use language such as "observed", "associated with", "promising",
or "inconclusive" rather than causal language unless a suitable design supports
it.

## Decision options

- continue;
- adapt;
- pause;
- stop;
- insufficient evidence.

Human review remains mandatory for every measurement.
