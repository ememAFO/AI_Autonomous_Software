# Direct Interview Guide

## Format

- 20–25 minutes.
- Semi-structured.
- One interviewer and one target-market participant.
- No customer-identifying information.
- No recording unless a separate approved process exists.
- Capture a sanitised summary, not a full transcript.
- Ask for a recent real example before asking hypothetical questions.

## Opening

> Thank you for speaking with me. I am researching how UK electrical businesses
> manage quotations after they are sent. I am not testing your knowledge and I
> am not asking you to share customer names, contact details, addresses or
> documents. I will capture a short anonymised summary. You can decline any
> question or stop at any time.

Confirm:

- participant is currently responsible for or closely involved in quotations;
- UK electrical-trade context;
- consent version;
- permission to use a sanitised summary for product-discovery research.

## Section 1 — Current workflow

1. Talk me through what normally happens from receiving an enquiry to recording
   the final quotation outcome.
2. Which systems, spreadsheets, messages or notes are involved?
3. Who is responsible for follow-up?
4. At what point is a quotation considered closed?

## Section 2 — Recent real example

5. Think about a recent quotation whose outcome was not immediately clear. What
   happened?
6. What did you know, and what remained unknown?
7. Did you follow up? Why then?
8. What happened after the follow-up?
9. Was the final outcome recorded anywhere?

Do not request names, addresses, phone numbers, exact quotation documents, or
customer messages.

## Section 3 — Problem magnitude

10. How often does this happen?
11. Which types of job are most affected?
12. What does an unclassified outcome cost in time, planning confidence or lost
    learning?
13. When is non-response not a problem?

## Section 4 — Existing alternatives

14. Which existing software features could already help?
15. Why are those features used or not used?
16. What manual workaround works well?
17. What would make another tool unnecessary?

## Section 5 — Decision support

18. What would help you decide which quotation deserves attention?
19. What would make a reminder inappropriate or annoying?
20. Which stop rules would you expect?
21. Which outcomes and loss reasons are worth recording?
22. Who should approve any customer-facing message?

## Section 6 — Concrete action

23. Would you review a simple workflow using your existing tools?
24. Would you share anonymised baseline counts for a small pilot?
25. What would need to be true before you would pay?
26. What price or commitment would feel unreasonable?

Do not turn hypothetical enthusiasm into willingness-to-pay evidence. Only a
completed qualifying action enters that lane.

## Closing challenge

27. What is the strongest reason this idea would fail for businesses like yours?
28. Which type of contractor should we not build this for?
29. What have I misunderstood?

## Required interviewer summary

The sanitised summary must distinguish:

- observed participant behaviour;
- participant statement;
- interviewer inference;
- supporting evidence;
- opposing evidence;
- unanswered questions;
- concrete action completed or not completed.
