# Public Post Options

Only one version should be approved and published at a time.

## Option A — Neutral discovery post

**UK electricians and electrical contractors — quick research question**

When you send a quotation, how easy is it to tell what eventually happened?

I am researching how small electrical businesses manage quotes that are still
open, declined, delayed, accepted elsewhere, or never formally closed.

This is not a sales pitch and I am not building another automatic reminder
tool. I want to understand the real workflow first:

- how quotation outcomes are currently recorded;
- why some quotes remain unclassified;
- what makes follow-up useful or annoying;
- what information would help you decide which quote needs attention.

The research form does not ask for your name, email, phone number, customer
details, or business address. It takes around three minutes.

A separate optional contact form will be available for anyone open to a short
research conversation or a small pilot.

**Human approval required before adding the form URL or publishing.**

## Option B — Problem-led post

**Do some of your electrical quotes simply go quiet?**

Not necessarily rejected. Not accepted. Just left without a clear outcome.

I am researching whether this creates a real admin or revenue-visibility problem
for UK electricians and small electrical contractors.

The aim is not to send more automated messages. It is to understand whether a
simple decision-support process could help businesses:

- confirm whether a quote was received;
- decide which quotes need attention;
- record accepted, declined, deferred and expired outcomes;
- stop chasing when follow-up is no longer useful;
- understand why work is being lost.

The anonymous research form takes around three minutes and does not request
contact or customer information.

**Human approval required before adding the form URL or publishing.**

## Option C — Pilot recruitment post

**Looking for a small number of UK electrical contractors to test a quote-outcome workflow**

The pilot is for businesses that issue quotations but cannot always see whether
the job was accepted, declined, delayed, awarded elsewhere, or simply left open.

The test will use your existing quoting process. It will not automatically
message customers or replace your current software.

The pilot will examine:

- your baseline quotation outcomes;
- which quotes genuinely require attention;
- whether a structured follow-up process improves classification;
- how much manual time it adds or saves;
- whether any improvement lasts.

Participants must be willing to share anonymised counts and outcomes. Customer
names, contact details, addresses and quotation documents are not required.

**Human approval required before publication, participant selection, or contact.**
