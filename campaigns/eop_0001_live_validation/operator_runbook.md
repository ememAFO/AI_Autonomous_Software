# Campaign Operator Runbook

## Before launch

1. Review every public-facing sentence.
2. Select one public post.
3. Implement the provider-neutral research form.
4. Implement a separate optional contact form.
5. Confirm no direct identifiers enter the research response.
6. Confirm the active Stage 4G campaign ID, consent version and question-set
   version.
7. Test the response-token flow.
8. Test that prohibited fields and placeholder data fail closed.
9. Complete the launch approval template.
10. Keep all protected launch actions false until explicit human approval.

## During public discovery

- publish only the approved text;
- do not edit the live questions without versioning;
- do not debate or persuade respondents;
- do not convert comments into research records without consent;
- retain negative responses;
- screen non-target participants;
- review free text for identifiers before capture;
- do not treat expressions of interest as completed actions.

## Interview selection

Prefer variation across:

- self-employed and small firms;
- quotation volumes;
- UK nations;
- manual and software-supported workflows;
- positive, neutral and sceptical respondents.

Do not select only enthusiastic participants.

## Interview operation

- confirm eligibility and consent;
- use the guide;
- ask for a recent real example;
- avoid leading language;
- separate statement, observation and inference;
- record opposing evidence;
- create only a sanitised summary;
- route the summary through Stage 4G review.

## Pilot selection

A pilot participant should:

- issue enough quotations for a meaningful baseline;
- be willing to use bounded outcome categories;
- approve all customer-facing actions;
- share anonymised counts;
- accept that a negative result will be retained.

## Incident handling

Pause capture when:

- direct identifiers enter the research dataset;
- the consent version is wrong;
- participant eligibility cannot be established;
- duplicate or synthetic submissions appear;
- public discussion exposes customer information;
- an operator attempts to bypass human review;
- a pilot requests autonomous customer messaging.

## End-of-cycle review

Record:

- response volume;
- target-market eligibility;
- hold-only public responses;
- interviews completed;
- concrete actions completed;
- opposing evidence;
- pilot enrolment;
- baseline availability;
- measurement quality;
- confounders;
- next decision.
