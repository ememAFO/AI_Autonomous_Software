# Research Consent and Data-Minimisation Notice

## Plain-language notice

We are researching how UK electricians and small electrical contractors manage
quotation outcomes and follow-up.

Participation is voluntary. The research is intended to guide product and
workflow decisions. It is not legal, financial, regulatory or business advice.

Do not provide:

- your name in the research response;
- email address or phone number;
- business or customer address;
- customer names or contact information;
- quotation documents;
- message transcripts;
- payment information;
- login credentials;
- health or other special-category personal data.

Research responses and optional contact details are stored separately. A random
participant token may be used to connect an opt-in action with an anonymised
research response.

Public-form responses may inform discovery, but they are not automatically
treated as customer interviews or validated evidence.

The project may retain anonymised summaries, opposing evidence and aggregate
results for audit and product-development purposes.

## Consent checkbox

> I understand the research purpose, I have not included direct identifiers or
> customer details, and I agree that my anonymised response may be reviewed for
> product-discovery research.

## Interview confirmation

Before an interview, confirm:

- the participant is acting voluntarily;
- the interviewer will record only a sanitised summary;
- the participant may skip questions or stop;
- no customer-identifying information is required;
- a concrete action is recorded separately from an opinion;
- the active consent version.

## Contact separation

The optional contact form must:

- be hosted separately from the research form;
- collect only what is necessary to arrange the selected action;
- store the generated participant token;
- avoid copying contact details into Stage 4G capture;
- have its own retention and deletion process.
