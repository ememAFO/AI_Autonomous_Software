# EOP-0001 Live Validation Campaign v0.1

## Purpose

Validate whether UK electricians and small electrical contractors experience
meaningful quotation-outcome uncertainty, whether they need help deciding which
quotes require attention, and whether a governed quote-outcome workflow creates
enough value to justify continued development.

This package is a campaign design and launch-control artefact. It does not
publish content, contact participants, change validation state, or import
evidence automatically.

## Positioning

The campaign is not testing another generic reminder product.

It is testing a governed quote-outcome decision-support workflow that may help
contractors:

- reduce unclassified quotation outcomes;
- record why work was won, lost, deferred, or left open;
- prioritise which quotations need attention;
- stop unnecessary follow-up;
- understand whether follow-up changes measurable outcomes;
- use existing software and manual workflows before building new software.

## Evidence lanes

| Lane | Purpose | Stage 4G treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Public structured response | Discover language, workflow and pain signals | Hold-only |
| Direct interview | Human-attested first-party qualitative evidence | `customer_interview` |
| Waitlist or pilot action | Concrete intent action | `waitlist_signup` |
| Willingness-to-pay action | Concrete commercial intent | `willingness_to_pay` |
| Operational pilot measurement | Measure baseline, intervention and persistence | Separate Stage 4G v0.2 lane; never relabelled as an interview |

## Non-negotiable controls

- No automated public posting.
- No automated participant contact.
- No customer messaging sent by the pilot without contractor approval.
- No research response may contain name, email, phone number, address, postcode,
  IP address, recording, or raw transcript.
- Contact details must be stored separately from research responses.
- Public-form responses do not become customer interviews.
- Pilot telemetry does not enter the validation evidence log.
- Negative and opposing evidence must be retained.
- No claim of causal impact without a defensible comparison design.
- No validation-state promotion without the existing human review gate.

## Recommended sequence

1. Approve the launch materials.
2. Implement the provider-neutral public form.
3. Test consent and contact separation.
4. Publish one public discovery post.
5. Review structured responses.
6. Invite suitable volunteers to direct interviews through the separate contact
   process.
7. Recruit a small pilot cohort.
8. capture baseline measures;
9. run the human-gated intervention;
10. measure persistence;
11. review and import only eligible first-party evidence through Stage 4G.
